FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are photos deleted immediately when I swipe?

No. Photos you swipe left are only added to a deletion list first. You review the list and confirm before anything is removed.

Can I restore a photo?

Yes. As long as a photo is still on the app’s deletion list, you can restore it at any time.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Delete or Keep does not upload your photos to external servers.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 18.2 or later. The app is made for iPhone and available in English and German.

Is there a subscription?

No. It’s a one-time purchase with no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases.
